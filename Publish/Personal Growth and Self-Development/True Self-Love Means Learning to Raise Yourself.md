I used to think that loving myself meant buying myself nice clothes, eating delicious food, and doing everything I could to make up for what I didn't receive as a child.

But this year, I've gradually come to realize that this isn't necessarily what self-love means. Sometimes, it's more like spoiling yourself.

So, what does it really mean to love yourself?

I think it means learning to play different roles in your own life.

Be your own teacher. Encourage yourself to learn and keep growing.

Be your own parent. Re-educate yourself and learn how to raise yourself all over again.

Be your own friend. Listen to yourself patiently and accept your emotions.

And be your own partner. Just like in traditional wedding vows, promise to love and cherish yourself through poverty and wealth, in sickness and in health, through good times and bad, for as long as you live.

There's one more thing I think is particularly important:

**Don't shift the responsibility for your own healing onto someone else.**

I believe many of us, especially when it comes to intimate relationships, have experienced something similar.

Perhaps we didn't receive enough love as children. So, as adults, we long to find someone who truly loves us through a romantic relationship.

We hope that this person will understand us, take care of us, and even make up for the love we missed out on during childhood.

But here's the question: Can you really control another person?

Today, they may promise to stay by your side. Tomorrow, they may choose to walk away.

If we place all our hopes for happiness in another person, we may end up hurting even more when they leave.

And honestly, no one can be expected to fulfill all of another person's emotional needs forever.

That's why I've come to believe that true personal growth begins with learning how to love yourself.

You have to take the initiative to change, guide, and encourage yourself. You have to accept your own imperfections and learn to accept the imperfections of those around you.

Don't expect someone else to do for you what you should be doing for yourself.

Before learning how to love others, please learn how to love yourself.

Because you are the person who will be with you throughout your entire life.

I want to dedicate these words to all of you. And I hope that, little by little, we can all become people who know how to take care of ourselves, understand ourselves, accept ourselves, and truly love ourselves.

**Keep going.**

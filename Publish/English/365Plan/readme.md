# Project Introduction: English Literature 365

## About the Project

**English Literature 365** is a year-long English literature learning plan, co-founded and carried out by me and my friend Florine. Our goal is simple: read a little English literature every day, and conquer the "hard-to-understand" words one by one — not by memorizing dictionary definitions, but by understanding them in context, feeling them, and truly making them our own.

This project is at once a reading plan, a vocabulary archive, and a journey of two people persisting, staying curious, and growing together.

## About Florine

Florine is from London and is an artist. She is kind-hearted, elegant and gracious, humorous and fun, genuinely curious about everything new, and at the same time holds clear and firm principles of her own. Being around her makes the world feel both gentle and wide open — she always finds beauty in ordinary things that others overlook, and always holds fast to what she believes matters, even amid easy laughter.

## How We Met

I first met Florine at an art café in Dresden. We are both frugal, yet we hold boundless artistic longing for life — and perhaps that is exactly what made us click instantly. Two thrifty people, talking about poetry, painting, and all those "impractical" beautiful things, with light in our eyes.

## How We Work Together

We have a clear division of roles:

- **Florine — the Teacher**: She reads the daily literary passage and picks out the difficult, subtle, or easily misunderstood words — the ones that trip learners up. As a Londoner and an artist with a sharp feel for language, she knows exactly which words deserve our attention.
- **Me — the Learner**: I study the words she selects, and then put them to work by **making sentences from real-life scenarios** — everyday situations, conversations, and moments from my own life. This way, each word leaves the dictionary and enters the world.

Together, we turn reading into understanding, and understanding into use.

## What We Will Do

- **Daily Reading**: Each day we select a passage of English literature (poetry, fiction, essays, short stories), progressing step by step from classics to contemporary works.
- **Word Selection**: Florine extracts the difficult words and provides contextual notes and explanations.
- **Life-Scenario Sentences**: I learn each word and write my own sentences set in everyday life, so the vocabulary becomes genuinely mine.
- **Shared Records**: We take turns making notes and explaining things to each other, turning each day's gains into a literary dictionary that belongs to the two of us.

After a year, we hope to have more than a vocabulary list — we hope to have a sharper and more composed way of seeing, nourished by literature — and a friendship worth remembering.


  

**第一页词汇 **

  

|**英文**|**中文释义**|**口语例句**|**解释与用法小贴士**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Pranayama**|调息法|I do a bit of pranayama in the morning to wake myself up.|瑜伽里的专用词，指通过控制呼吸来放松或提神的技巧。|
|**Nirvana**|极乐；解脱|Sitting on the couch with snacks after a long day is absolute nirvana.|原指佛教的“涅槃”，口语里用来形容极其舒服、爽爆了的状态。|
|**yab**|雅布|They had this super mysterious yab-yum statue in the gallery.|常用在藏传佛教密宗“双修（Yab-Yum）”的表达中。|
|**pothos**|绿萝|I got a pothos because it’s basically impossible to kill.|最常见的室内绿植，特别好养活。|
|**inextricable**|密不可分的|Coffee and my morning routine are inextricable at this point.|形容两件事像缠在一起的绳子，完全拆不开。|
|**lineage**|血统；世系|She can trace her lineage all the way back to the 1800s.|指家族的族谱、血脉或传承。|
|**radiance**|容光焕发|Her skin has this amazing radiance since she started sleeping early.|常用来形容皮肤有光泽、气色好。|
|**combian**|哥伦比亚的|Have you ever tried authentic Combian coffee? It’s super rich.|通常为 Colombian 的口语简称或拼写变体。|
|**acclimate**|适应环境|Give me a couple of days to acclimate to the time difference.|指身体或心理慢慢习惯新环境、新气候或时差。|
|**speedometer**|速度表|Keep an eye on the speedometer, there’s a speed trap ahead.|汽车仪表盘上的车速表。|
|**enforcement**|执行；执法|They’re pretty strict about parking enforcement around here.|常指警察或城管“抓违章/执法”的动作。|
|**perogies**|波兰饺子|I just microwaved some frozen perogies for a quick lunch.|欧洲很常见的一种土豆馅或肉馅半月形饺子。|
|**eternity**|永恒；极长时间|Waiting in this line feels like it’s taking an eternity.|口语里夸张表达“等了超级无敌久”。|
|**glimpse**|瞟一眼|I only got a quick glimpse of the cat before it ran under the bed.|指没仔细看清，只看了一眼、瞥了一下。|
|**unrequited**|单相思的|Ah, high school crushes are mostly just unrequited love, right?|常用于 _unrequited love_，表示单恋、落花有意流水无情。|
|**animal euthanasia**|动物安乐死|Putting a sick pet through animal euthanasia is always heartbreaking.|指兽医为痛苦的重病宠物实施安乐死。|
|**funsies**|为了好玩|We didn't plan it, we just drove out there for funsies.|非常地道的口语短语 _for funsies_，意思是“没啥正经目的，纯玩儿”。|
|**hung over**|宿醉的|I’m so hung over today, I need a giant bottle of water.|喝完酒第二天头疼恶心、喝大了的状态（也常拼作 hungover）。|
|**unhinged**|精神失常的|That guy on the train was acting completely unhinged.|俚语，形容某人举止疯狂、不正常、像疯子一样。|
|**spayed**|已绝育（母）|We took our puppy to the vet yesterday to get her spayed.|专门指**母的**动物做了绝育手术。|
|**syndrome**|综合征|Imposter syndrome is super common when starting a new job.|比如“冒充者综合征”（总觉得自己的成就全靠运气）。|
|**totality**|全体；全食|You really need special glasses to watch the solar eclipse in totality.|天文学中指日全食或月全食完全覆盖的那一刻。|
|**spells**|咒语|She loves reading stories about magic spells and witches.|魔法世界里的拼写咒语。|
|**articulacy**|表达能力|I really envy his articulacy; he always knows what to say.|指口才好、能非常清楚连贯地表达思想。|
|**corny**|陈腐的；俗气的|Dad jokes are always so corny, but I still laugh.|口语极高频词，形容笑话或老套话“很土、很老掉牙”。|
|**runway**|跑道；T台|The plane has been waiting on the runway for half an hour.|可以是飞机的起降跑道，也可以是模特走秀的T台。|
|**archetype**|原型；典型|He’s the classic archetype of a tech geek.|指某种人或事物最原始、最典型的模板。|
|**glamorous**|迷人的；光鲜的|Traveling for work isn't as glamorous as people think.|指看起来很高端、上档次、风光无限的样子。|
|**cinematic**|像电影一样的|The sunset tonight looked straight-up cinematic.|形容画面很唯美，像电影镜头拍出来的一样。|
|**pinching (myself)**|捏自己|I had to keep pinching myself to make sure I wasn't dreaming.|常用短语 _pinch myself_，指遇到了太好的事不敢相信。|
|**"protagonist"**|主角|She definitely acts like she’s the main protagonist of the world.|网络流行语“主角光环/主角综合征”里用的词。|
|**blazers logos / neon lights**|西装标识/霓虹灯|The bar was super retro with neon lights and cool blazers logos on the wall.|描述复古酒吧墙上的霓虹灯管或西装外套品牌标志。|
|**sustain**|维持|I don't think I can sustain this level of energy all day.|指把某种状态、精力或速度一直保持下去。|
|**Mediterranean**|地中海的|I’m really craving some fresh Mediterranean food for dinner.|经常用来指地中海风味的饮食（橄榄油、海鲜等）。|
|**ingrain**|根深蒂固|My mom tried to ingrain good manners in us from day one.|把某种观念或习惯打从小深刻在脑海里。|
|**prerogative**|独有的权利|It’s your prerogative to change your mind whenever you want.|口语常说“这是你的特权/权利，你想怎样就怎样”。|
|**mural**|大型壁画|Look at that giant mural painted on the side of the building!|画在大楼侧墙或街头墙面上的大型涂鸦壁画。|
|**Sesame**|芝麻|Can you get some sesame oil from the grocery store?|常见的食用原料（如 sesame oil 芝麻油）。|

|**英文**|**中文释义**|**口语例句**|**解释与用法小贴士**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Skull**|头骨|I bumped my head and I think I bruised my skull.|指脑袋外壳的骨头。|
|**national anthem**|国歌|Everyone stood up when they played the national anthem before the game.|体育比赛开场前播放的国歌。|
|**Political propaganda**|政治宣传|I try not to pay too much attention to political propaganda on social media.|指带有强烈政治倾向的信息洗脑或宣传。|
|**Scanded**|标有重音的|The poem was scanned closely for its rhythmic pattern.|分析诗歌韵律节奏时的专用语（手写稿可能为 scanned 的误写）。|
|**Exposé**|曝光报道|Did you watch that news exposé on the food industry last night?|电视或报纸上专门揭露丑闻、内幕的深度报道。|
|**Start genocides**|发起种族灭绝|Learning history helps prevent evil leaders from starting genocides ever again.|历史学词汇，指残酷灭绝某一族群的罪行。|
|**Scandal**|丑闻|That celebrity got caught in a huge scandal this week.|八卦新闻里明星或政客的翻车丑闻。|
|**Ethnic group**|少数民族/族群|The festival celebrates different traditions from various ethnic groups.|指具有相同文化背景或血统的民族群体。|
|**prejudice (bias)**|偏见|Try to keep an open mind without any prejudice against new ideas.|在没有了解事实前就带着有色眼镜看人或看事。|
|**Civilians**|平民|Innocent civilians shouldn't be harmed in any conflicts.|相对于军人、警察而言的普通老百姓。|
|**Cramp**|抽筋|Ugh, I got a super bad leg cramp in the middle of the night.|运动后或晚上睡觉时肌肉突然剧烈疼痛的抽筋。|
|**strain (pressure)**|负担；拉伤|Staring at the screen all day puts a lot of strain on your eyes.|可以指眼睛/肌肉的过度疲劳，也可以指精神压力。|
|**Skid**|打滑|The car started to skid on the icy road, which freaked me out.|车轮在湿滑或下雪路面上失去抓地力滑动。|
|**Escalator**|自动扶梯|Let's just take the escalator instead of the stairs.|商场里上下移动的电梯（区别于箱式升降梯 elevator）。|
|**aerial (Antenna)**|电视天线|The TV signal is weak because the aerial on the roof is bent.|英式英语里常用 aerial 表示房屋顶上的接收天线。|
|**bandaid (plaster)**|创可贴|Do you have a bandaid? I cut my finger on the paper.|美语叫 bandaid，英语叫 plaster，贴小伤口用的。|
|**Proactive**|积极主动的|You need to be proactive and ask for help if you're stuck.|不等别人发指令，自己提前采取行动。|
|**wiggle**|扭动|Can you wiggle your toes in those tight shoes?|稍微幅度小地摆动（比如摆动手指、脚趾、尾巴）。|
|**Sarcastic**|说话阴阳怪气的|Don't be so sarcastic, I was just asking a serious question!|说话带刺、嘲讽、挖苦人的语气。|
|**warranty**|保修期|My phone broke right after the warranty expired, typical!|电子产品或家电买来后的售后保修服务。|
|**circulation**|血液循环|Walking around helps get the blood circulation going in your legs.|身体里血液的流动循环。|
|**crumple down**|瘫倒|He just crumpled down onto the couch after working a 12-hour shift.|像一张纸被揉成一团一样无力地倒在地上或沙发上。|
|**mime acting**|默剧/哑剧|Mime acting is tough because you can't use any words.|靠肢体和面部表情、不说一句话的艺术表演。|
|**improvisation**|即兴发挥|We ran out of ingredients, so dinner was a complete improvisation.|事先没准备，根据现场情况临时胡乱凑数或表演。|
|**elixir**|神药|A hot cup of tea is the ultimate elixir for a sore throat.|口语用来指“解决某种麻烦的绝佳妙方”。|
|**persistent**|持续不断的|If the headache is persistent, you should probably see a doctor.|形容头痛、咳嗽等症状连续好几天都不消退。|
|**perseverant**|不屈不挠的|She’s super perseverant and never gives up, no matter how tough it gets.|形容性格非常有韧劲，遇到困难咬牙坚持。|
|**hobble**|瘸着走|I had to hobble back home after twisting my ankle during the run.|因为脚崴了或脚痛，一脚深一脚浅地走路。|
|**diligent**|勤奋踏实的|If you stay diligent, your English will improve really fast.|形容学习或工作非常努力、不偷懒。|
|**flourishing**|蓬勃发展的|Her online coffee business is really flourishing these days.|指生意做得风生水起、蒸蒸日上。|
|**conscientious**|认真的；有责任心的|He’s very conscientious and always double-checks his work.|办事极其细心、靠谱，不会敷衍了事。|
|**back burner**|暂时搁置|Let's put this project on the back burner for now and focus on the deadline.|常用短语 _on the back burner_，指把不急的事先凉一边。|
|**ungrateful**|忘恩负义的|I bought him lunch and he didn't even say thanks, so ungrateful!|俗称“白眼狼”，别人帮了他他连句谢都没有。|
|**superb**|极好的|The coffee at this new cafe is absolutely superb.|口语里高级的夸奖词，相当于 excellent/amazing。|

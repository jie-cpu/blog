One principle I want to live by is: **be agreeable in attitude, but independent in action.**

Not every disagreement deserves an argument. Winning an emotional battle often costs more than it gives. You may prove your point, but lose your peace of mind, damage a relationship, and gain nothing in return.

Stop overanalysing other people's behaviour. Why are they distant? Why haven't they replied? Why don't they seem to care? Sometimes, their actions are already enough of an answer. You don't need to understand everyone's intentions.

Be kind, but maintain your boundaries. Be understanding, but never sacrifice your own interests just to keep a relationship. If someone repeatedly makes you feel unwanted or disrespected, learn to walk away.

**You don't have to win every argument, explain everything, or earn everyone's approval.**

Stay calm, protect your energy, focus on what you can control, and never lose yourself trying to please others.

**Go along without giving in. Stay peaceful without compromising yourself.**

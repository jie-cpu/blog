Life is a constant process of learning to accept that I cannot control everything.

I cannot control what other people think of me, how they perceive me, or what they choose to say about me. But I can control my own actions, my decisions, and the way I choose to live my life.

I'm someone who tends to overthink things. I always want to make the right choices, not simply because of how they might be perceived by others, but because I understand that this is my life. One day, perhaps twenty years from now, if I'm lucky, I'll look back on the life I've lived. And I hope I can feel proud of the choices I've made.

It's not always easy, especially when you're young and feel as though every move you make is being magnified and scrutinized by others.

So, perhaps all we can do is try our best to make thoughtful choices and focus on what is within our control.

At the end of the day, we may not be able to decide how others see us, but we can decide who we want to become and what kind of life we want to live.

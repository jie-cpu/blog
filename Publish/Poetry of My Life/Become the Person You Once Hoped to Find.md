Perhaps one of the most important things we need to learn in life is how to turn our expectations of others into expectations of ourselves.

We often hope that someone will understand us, stay by our side, offer us warmth, and give us the love and reassurance we long for. We wish others would treat us in the way we want to be treated, and sometimes, when those expectations are not met, we end up feeling disappointed.

But perhaps there is another way to approach life.

Instead of waiting for someone to understand us, we can learn to understand ourselves. Instead of waiting for someone to show us kindness, we can first learn to be kind to ourselves. Instead of hoping someone will become our source of strength, we can work towards becoming someone we can rely on.

This doesn’t mean we should stop needing love or become so independent that we no longer need anyone. It simply means understanding that our worth should not be defined by someone else’s choices, and our happiness should not depend entirely on someone else’s response.

Above all, we need to believe in ourselves.

Believe that we can become the person we aspire to be. Believe that we are worthy of the things we hope for. And believe that even the most distant dreams can gradually become reality through small but consistent steps.

Because what we believe in shapes the direction in which we grow. The way we see ourselves influences the person we eventually become.

So, don’t place all your expectations on other people.

Bring those expectations back to yourself. Take ownership of your choices. Continue to love sincerely, to hope courageously, and to accept the things beyond your control.

May we all eventually become the person we once hoped someone else would be for us.

**Because true growth is not about finally finding someone who can fulfil all our expectations. It is about becoming that person ourselves.**
